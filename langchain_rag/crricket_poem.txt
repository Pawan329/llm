On dusty fields or emerald green,
Where hopes are high and eyes are keen,
A bat swings firm, a ball takes flight,
And hearts beat fast in pure delight.

The bowler runs with silent fire,
Each step a promise, fierce desire,
The seam spins tales through open air,
A test of skill, of nerve, of care.

The batter stands, both calm and bold,
A story yet to be retold,
One perfect shot through cover drives,
And suddenly the crowd comes alive.

The sound of leather kissing wood,
A moment frozen, understood,
A cheer erupts, a boundary scored,
In that instant, joy is poured.

From gully lanes to stadium lights,
From childhood games to grandest nights,
Cricket lives in every soul,
Not just a sport—but something whole.

For in each run and every fall,
There lies a lesson for us all—
To stand, to strive, to take the chance,
And play life’s game with fearless stance.
